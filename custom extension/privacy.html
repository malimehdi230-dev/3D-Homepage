Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12  ·  Extension version: 2.2.0

1. Your local preferences

3D Parallax New Tab stores your name, themes, shortcuts, headline, layout, notes, tasks, search history, and AI provider keys on your device, using chrome.storage.local and localStorage. Uploaded wallpaper files are stored locally in IndexedDB.

The extension does not upload your saved preferences automatically. Optional weather, AI, web-search, wallpaper-search, and feedback features make the network requests described below. Exporting a backup creates a file on your device; saved API keys are excluded unless you choose to include them in the homepage settings.

2. Permissions we use — and why

  • storage / unlimitedStorage — saves your settings and wallpaper blob locally.
  • Geolocation (optional, browser-level prompt) — fetches weather from the public Open-Meteo API using your coordinates. Your coordinates are sent directly from your browser to Open-Meteo; we do not see or store them.

3. Third-party network requests

The extension may make the following outbound requests on your behalf:

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) — free, open-source weather API. No account or API key required. See their terms.
  • Groq or your chosen compatible provider — receives your API key and requests when you use AI chat, AI headlines, AI Makeover, or provider tests. Chat requests include recent messages; styling requests include your style prompt.
  • DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia — receive your search query when AI web search is enabled. Results may be included in the request to your AI provider.
  • Wallhaven and wallpaper image hosts — receive wallpaper search terms and image requests when an AI styling or imported setup requests a remote wallpaper.
  • Your selected search engine or shortcut website — receives the normal browser navigation when you search or open a shortcut.
  • The developer's feedback service (3-d-parallax-backend.vercel.app) — receives the message, feedback category, optional rating, selected theme, extension version, and timestamp when you submit the feedback form. Network services may also receive normal connection information such as your IP address.

No tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks are included.

4. AI provider keys

If you add an API key, it is stored on this device and sent to the provider endpoint you configure when using or testing AI features. You can remove keys in Settings → AI features. Backup files that you explicitly choose to export with API keys contain those keys; keep such files private.

5. Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and bump the extension version. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

For questions or concerns, open Settings → Backup & help → Send Feedback in the extension. The publisher's contact information is also available on the Chrome Web Store listing once published.
