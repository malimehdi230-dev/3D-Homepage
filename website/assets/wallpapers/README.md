# Website wallpaper previews

Original scenic imagery generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the public website's wallpaper illustrations. The photos and library cards show example backgrounds; these files do not seed or change the developer-managed wallpaper catalog.

Project assets: `forest-lake.webp`, `coastal-sunset.webp`, `desert-dunes.webp`. WebP exports are 810 × 1080 pixels, sized for below-the-fold portrait cards and gallery thumbnails. The original PNG files are retained in the workspace under `.qa/website-images/`.

## Generation prompts

### forest-lake

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original example wallpaper photograph for a browser extension website. Create one beautiful vertical 3:4 landscape photograph of a still alpine lake with tall evergreen trees framing its edges, a gentle misty mountain ridge in the distance, a pale soft morning sky, mirrored forest reflections in the water. Tasteful muted forest green and cool sage tones, natural photographic texture, quiet cinematic light with realistic detail and crisp silhouettes. Composition should read clearly when cropped to a small portrait card, with water and reflections in the lower half and the mountain visible in the upper third. No people, buildings, signage, interface, typography, logos, watermark, border or mockup. This is a finished photo-like wallpaper background, not a website screen.

### coastal-sunset

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original sample wallpaper for a personal Chrome homepage website. One vertical 3:4 natural landscape photograph of a quiet coastal cove at sunset, deep blue-green ocean, pale warm sand, a rugged headland on the horizon and soft peach sunset light. Elevated shore viewpoint, elegantly balanced broad shapes readable at thumbnail size, authentic photographic detail and calm editorial color grading. Keep clear sky in the upper third. Natural peaceful atmosphere. No people, text, signs, boats, logos, watermark, frame, interface or device mockup. Single finished scenic image only.

### desert-dunes

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original sample browser wallpaper landscape photo. One vertical 3:4 photographic scene of sculptural sand dunes in late afternoon light, flowing layered ridgelines, warm ochre sand, soft clay-colored shadows and a pale dusty blue sky. Elegant broad curves, realistic fine sand texture and natural light, refined muted colors suited to a green and off-white software website. A few receding dunes give depth. Clear composition that remains recognizable at tiny thumbnail size. No text, people, vehicles, animals, logos, watermarks, interface, border or device. Return only the finished landscape image.
