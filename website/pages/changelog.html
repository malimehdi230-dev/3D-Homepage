Updates to the extension and the services that support it.

2.6.0October 14, 2026Prepared for Chrome Web Store submission

A toolbar that follows your homepage.

A compact popup reflects your current setup, with the new layered-P icon throughout the extension.

  • See your actual name, theme or wallpaper, shortcuts, sound choice and layout.
  • Open the relevant settings section straight from the toolbar.
  • Use Feedback to open the extension’s store reviews page.
  • Send private reports through Backup & help → Report a problem.
  • Open Groq’s key console with Get Groq API in provider settings.
  • Keep existing preferences and wallpapers with no new permissions.
2.5.0October 13, 2026Published on the Chrome Web Store

Feedback that’s easier to share.

A more readable feedback form with clearer controls, privacy and support links.

  • Keep an unsent draft when reloading the same tab.
  • Clear an optional rating before submitting.
  • Retry a failed request without creating duplicate feedback.
  • Keep the submission reference shown after success.
  • Publishers can review feedback, track progress, set priorities and keep private notes.

Centered alignment, responsive widget content, five saved wallpapers and the dynamic wallpaper library are included in this release.

2.4.0Previous development release

A better place for your backgrounds.

  • One picker for built-in themes, saved wallpapers and the online library.
  • Keep up to five local images or videos, with saved wallpaper migration.
  • Switch themes without losing your wallpaper collection.
  • Search, download and save published library wallpapers for offline use.
  • Improved centered alignment, compact controls and readable system fonts.
