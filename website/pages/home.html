More help ↗
Is 3D Parallax free?

The extension is free. Optional AI features use your own provider key; that provider may charge for usage.

Can I use my own wallpaper?

Yes. Save up to five photos or videos on your device. Your collection stays saved when you switch to a built-in theme.

Will it work offline?

Saved wallpapers and ordinary local homepage features remain available offline. Online search, library discovery, weather and AI need an internet connection.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed for the ordinary homepage features. The separate publisher dashboard is used by the developer to manage the library and feedback.

What happens to my bookmarks and browsing history?

3D Parallax replaces the New Tab page. It does not read Chrome’s browsing history or import your Chrome bookmarks. You add the shortcuts you want yourself.
